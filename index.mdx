## What you can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Convert currencies" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Java 17">
    Ensure you have JDK 17 or later installed on your machine.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Gson dependency">
    Download `gson-2.11.0.jar` and place it on your classpath before compiling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compile the project">
    Compile all Java source files under `src/` with Gson on the classpath.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the application">
    Launch `com.AnthonyChuan.Conversor.main` and follow the interactive prompts to convert a currency.
  </Step>
</Steps>
